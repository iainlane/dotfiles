The backup uploader now passes GNU tar's `--numeric-owner` option. The failing
`--numeric-ids` spelling belongs to rsync, where the Hermes snapshot code uses
it correctly; passing it to tar stopped both backup paths before encryption or
upload.

I committed the focused fix as
`fix(backup): ask tar for numeric owners by its own name`.
`bash -n lib/r2-upload.sh` and the repository checks pass.
