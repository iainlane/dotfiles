The post-signing check in `pkg/sign.Bundle` always passed the DSSE envelope's
pre-authentication encoding to `verify.WithArtifact`. The verifier treats that
as the artifact and compares its digest against the subjects the in-toto
statement declares, so it was comparing the envelope with the things the
envelope is about. Those never match, and the two are not even the same kind of
value, so no correctly formed DSSE bundle could pass.

Reading the subject digest out of the statement and passing it as
`WithArtifactDigest` would not help. Both the envelope and its subjects come
from the same caller, so that check would compare the caller's input with
itself. `Bundle` now asks for verification without an artifact when the content
is DSSE, and keeps `WithArtifact` on the pre-authentication encoding for the
message-signature case where the bytes really are the signed artifact. The
comment above it records why: we have just signed this envelope trusting that
the caller referenced the artifacts they meant to, and the certificate identity
still comes from their identity token.

The trusted root is still consulted and the bundle is still verified before it
is returned; signing gained no new input. I committed the change as
`fix(sign): do not verify a DSSE envelope against its own subjects`;
`go build ./...` and `go vet ./pkg/...` pass, and the package tests are
unchanged and still green, though none of them exercises this path.
